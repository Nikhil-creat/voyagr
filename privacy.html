Privacy

VOYAGR runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no database and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Your API keys, saved trips, packing list and settings live in your browser storage. You can lock keys with the key vault or clear them at any time.

What is sent, and to whom

When you plan a trip, your inputs go straight from your device to the services that answer them: your chosen AI provider (Groq or Google Gemini), OpenStreetMap services for places and routes, Open-Meteo for weather, Wikipedia for place summaries and Frankfurter for exchange rates. Each has its own privacy policy. If the optional server proxy is enabled, your request passes through it to Groq and is not stored.

Location and microphone

Location is used only when you tap the 📍 button, and the microphone only when you tap 🎤. Photos you add for scene analysis are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Your choices

Clear your browser site data to remove everything VOYAGR stored.
